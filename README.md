# KEY4U: Your Dream Car Import

Business Name: KEY4U
Tagline: Easy, fast, affordable
Location: Sofia, Bulgaria
Languages: Bulgarian and English

Business Description:
KEY4U imports cars from Canada and the USA to Bulgaria. We handle the entire process from client request to final delivery — including transport, Tehnotest, light conversions, and registration. Clients simply tell us what car they want, and we take care of everything else. We focus on private buyers looking for affordable luxury and reliable service.

Main Selling Points:

Full end-to-end service (request → delivery → registration)

Affordable luxury cars

Personalized search and import

Local expertise in Bulgaria

Focus on trust, transparency, and quality

Target Audience:
Private individuals in Bulgaria (and potentially foreign buyers living in Bulgaria) who want luxury cars at reasonable prices, without handling import complexities themselves.

Brand Personality:
Trustworthy, premium, clean, professional.

Design Preferences:

Colors: dark green, white, black (I’ll add logo and exact shades later)

Style: sleek, minimal, premium

Visual focus: cars and design, not people or office photos

Layout should feel modern and credible

Website Structure:

Home Page – Hero section introducing the brand and tagline; highlight trust and full-service approach.

Import Process – Step-by-step explanation of how KEY4U imports cars from Canada and the USA to Bulgaria. Emphasize simplicity for the client.

Find My Car – Form where users can request a car. Fields: model, year, color, trim, and other preferences. Include call to action: “Find My Car”.

FAQ Section – Common questions about import times, costs, taxes, transport, Tehnotest, registration, etc.

Get in Touch – Contact options and social links.
